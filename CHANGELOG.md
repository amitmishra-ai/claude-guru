Footer: single `Share my impact` contained CTA + text `Preview share card` link — both open the existing `shareOpen` dialog where the full downloadable share-card preview and social buttons live. Dropped the three exploratory options (Hero Poster / Polaroid / Platform Gallery) and the unused `thumbScaleXs/Sm` helpers. Added `alpha` to the `@mui/material/styles` import.
